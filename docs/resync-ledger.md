# Resync ledger: reference `26e76d14` to `fa18e7fa`

This engine last matched the reference at `26e76d14` (measured 2026-09-09).
By 2026-11-08 the reference had moved to `fa18e7fa`: 280 approved goldens
(from 227), every one now asserting its recognized features, three new
rules, a knurl recognizer, a rebuilt marking recognizer, seam-aware
measurement, and rewrites of most of the hole, cavity, boss, slot, blend
and sheet recognizers.

The resync replays the reference's own history, oldest first, one commit at
a time. Each commit message states the intent and the C++ diff shows the
mechanism, so porting them in order keeps every intermediate state
explainable and the parity numbers attributable. Run
`tests/fixtures/check_parity.py` after each row.

**Scope.** Rows whose only in-scope files are listed below are ported.
Nesting, DXF, flat-pattern unfolding and fold sequencing, the HTTP server,
STEP metadata scans, GLB export and the `metrics` producer are out of scope
for this workbench, and the parts of a commit touching only them are skipped.
The goldens never assert `metrics` (the reference removed it from its drift
gate on 2026-10-02), so skipping the producer costs no parity.

**Kernel.** The goldens were approved under OpenCascade 8.0.0; FreeCAD ships
7.x. A difference traced to the kernel is recorded under the row that
exposed it and left, by ruling of 2026-11-08, rather than forced.

**Reference bugs.** 1:1 means matching the reference's behaviour, including
behaviour that looks wrong. Suspected reference bugs are noted in the row,
not "fixed" here.

**Outcome, 2026-11-09.** Every row is `done` or `n/a`, and all 280 reference
goldens at `fa18e7fa` match (`tests/fixtures/check_golden.py`: 280 of 280,
from 98 at the start of the resync; 182 fixed, none broken). The fixtures that
needed more than a commit replay, and why, are recorded in
`docs/parity-tasks.md` under "Residuals after the fa18e7fa resync".

Status: `todo`, `done`, `partial` (say what is missing), `n/a` (nothing in
